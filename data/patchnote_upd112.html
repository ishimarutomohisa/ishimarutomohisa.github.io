PATCH NOTES – UPDATE 112: FROZEN PART 4, ROBIN HOOD PART 2
2026/10/30
Patch Note

WHAT'S NEW

 

Step into the magical world of Disney’s Frozen and meet Oaken, the Duke of Weselton, and Grand Pabbie in the exciting Frozen Mini Event. Continue your Frozen journey with the Story Pass and unlock special Nightgown Anna and Elsa outfits to bring even more winter magic to your Kingdom.

 

A new adventure awaits in Disney Magic Kingdoms! Join Robin Hood on a journey through a new location filled with exciting challenges, hidden surprises, and a story waiting to be uncovered.

 

Download the update on November 3rd

Frozen Story Pass begins on November 5th

Frozen Mini Event begins on November 12th  

Robin Hood Adventure Event begins on November 27th **

 

*Note: Some platforms may receive the update at a later time, but don’t worry—everyone will be able to take part in our time-limited events at the same time!

**Note II: The start date for the Adventure is approximate and may change later in the update.

Robin Hood Adventure – November 27th – December 14th

 

The dates for the Adventure are approximate and may change later in the update.

 

Important Changes to Adventures: Due to game optimization requirements, we’ve had to adjust our original plans for previous Adventures, including the Mickey & Friends Adventure. Once the new Adventure begins, the previous Adventure will end permanently and will no longer be accessible. You have until November 27th to complete the Mickey & Friends Adventure and claim all its rewards. Please make sure to finish the Adventure before the new one begins, as you won’t be able to return to it afterward. We apologize for any inconvenience these changes may cause and thank you for your understanding.

 

In the new Robin Hood Adventure you'll help Robin Hood, Little John, and Maid Marian foil the Sheriff's insidious plans and defeat him in the archery tournament.

 

To participate in the event, you need to have Discovery 6 completed (welcoming Cinderella). The Adventure takes place on the map, separated from the Kingdom – you will see the portal leading to it near the Main Street.

  • You don’t have to have these characters welcomed or leveled up to a certain point to participate in the Adventure
  • Some parts of the map unlock only after completing certain quests
  • Complete quests and progress through the map
  • Spend Energy to clear paths and remove obstacles
    • Energy sources:
      • Time: 1 Energy every 210 seconds (3,5 minutes) until it reaches the limit of 100 Energy
      • Energy Bonus: 20 Energy every 8 hours
      • Event Special Orders in Sort the Magic mini-game
      • Milestones and Leaderboard rewards during the Tapper Event
      • Special Bundles in the Shop
      • Chain Offer
  • During your Adventure, you can find Chest Keys that you can use for opening the following chests on the Adventure map:
    • Common Pin Chest (1 key)
    • Uncommon Pin Chest (2 keys)

BALANCING CHANGES

 

Why do we rebalance the game? Disney Magic Kingdoms is a game with many interconnected systems. Since our initial launch, we have added a lot of new features and content, and each time we do this, it can significantly affect how these systems interact with each other. To ensure that the gameplay experience remains as intended, we are sometimes required to make balancing changes. Ultimately, rebalancing the game is necessary so that Disney Magic Kingdoms is fun and enjoyable for all. Thank you for your patience and understanding!

 

Sort the Magic: Special Orders

 

November 12th – November 27th Event Special Orders for Frozen Mini Event will be available every 8 hours. A maximum of 30 Event Special Orders can be completed during the event. Each Event Special Order rewards players with:

  • Orders 1, 2: 100 Event Currency, 1 Oaken’s Wool Cap 
    Orders 3, 4: 100 Event Currency, 1 Oaken Ears Hat
  • Orders 5-18: 100 Event Currency, 1 Frozen Relic
  • Orders 19-30: 100 Event Currency, 1 Lemon Ribbon Chest

 

November 30th – December 7th Event Special Orders for Cookie Party Event will be available every 8 hours. A maximum of 14 Event Special Orders can be completed during the event. Each Event Special Order rewards players with:

  • 500 Magic
  • 1 Gold Seal Red Chest (Containing Tea Cups)

 

Once all Event Special Orders have been completed, all subsequent Special Orders will revert to the standard version, rewarding 500 Magic and 1 Blue Ribbon Chest.

 

Please note that if you take a break from the mini-game or do not complete your Special Orders, your current Event Special Order will remain available with the same rewards until it is completed. You will receive new Event Special Orders only after claiming the current one.
